Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Photos (Count the Jar)

  • When you take or upload a photo, it is sent only to the server hosting the game -- typically a laptop on the same Wi-Fi network as you.
  • The photo is processed entirely in memory to count objects.
  • Photos are never written to disk, never logged, and never sent anywhere else. They are discarded as soon as your count is returned.

Scores and preferences

Leaderboards and game state live in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. Clear site data at any time to erase them.

Player names

Names typed into games exist only in browser memory during play. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Network activity

The app talks only to the server hosting it. No data leaves that local network unless the host deliberately exposes their server to the internet.
