# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 21, 2026**

Party Games is a self-hosted collection of offline party games. This policy explains exactly what happens to any data when you use it.

## The short version

We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

## What the software does with your data

### Photos (Count the Jar)

- When you take or upload a photo in Count the Jar, the image is sent from your browser to **the server you are connected to** -- which is typically a laptop on the same Wi-Fi network as you.
- The photo is processed entirely in memory to count the objects in it.
- Photos are **never written to disk, never logged, and never sent anywhere else**. They are discarded as soon as the count is returned.

### Game scores and preferences

- Charades team scores, Count the Jar leaderboards, and similar game state are stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. You can clear them at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

### Player names

- Names you type into games (Truth or Dare players, duel opponents, team names) exist only in your browser's memory during play. They are not transmitted to anyone.

## Network activity

The app makes requests only to the server hosting it. If you are playing at someone's party, that is their machine. No data leaves that local network unless the host has deliberately exposed their server to the internet.

## Children

The games contain no ads or tracking, but some Truth or Dare prompts are intended for adults. Hosts are responsible for choosing appropriate spice levels for their group.

## Changes

If this software ever gains online features that would change this policy, this document will be updated before those features ship.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the project repository.
